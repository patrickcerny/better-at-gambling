Questions at the cashier

FAQ

What is Better at Gambling?

Better at Gambling is an online casino party game for 2 to 8 friends. Everyone starts with $1,000 in play chips. You win money at eleven casino games such as blackjack, roulette and slots, in 16 minigames, or by shoving the leader and shaking the chips out of their pockets. The richest bean at closing time wins.

Is there real money gambling?

No. Better at Gambling is played with fictional chips only. No real money can be wagered, bought or won, and there are no microtransactions, no loot boxes and nothing to cash out.

How many players can play?

2 to 8 players online. You invite friends through Steam. A practice mode and a tutorial let you learn the tables alone.

Which platforms is it on?

Better at Gambling is coming to Steam for Windows PC. Builds for macOS and Linux are in testing.

When does Better at Gambling come out?

The game is coming soon to Steam. Add it to your Steam wishlist to get a message on release day.

Does it have voice chat?

Yes. Proximity voice chat is built in, so you hear the players near you, and the beans move their mouths when you talk. A microphone is optional.

How long is a round?

You set it: 1 to 10 minigames with 1 to 6 minutes of gambling in between. A standard night takes about half an hour.
